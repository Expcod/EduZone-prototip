Acid-Base Reactions

Acids and bases are two related classes of chemicals. When mixed, they undergo a neutralization reaction, producing water and a salt.

The pH scale measures how acidic or basic a solution is, ranging from 0 to 14:

  • pH < 7: Acidic
  • pH = 7: Neutral
  • pH > 7: Basic
{% elif simulation.experiment.subject.name == 'Fizika' %}
Electric Circuits

An electric circuit is a path in which electrons flow from a voltage or current source. Electric current flows when a voltage is applied across electrical conductors.

Ohm's Law states that the current through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage:

V = I × R

Where V is voltage, I is current, and R is resistance.

{% elif simulation.experiment.subject.name == 'Fizika' %}

According to Ohm's Law (V = I × R), when resistance remains constant, increasing the voltage will proportionally increase the current. This can be observed by the faster movement of electrons in the circuit.

When resistance increases, the current decreases (I = V/R). This results in less power being delivered to the bulb (P = I²R), making it dimmer.
